Kitchen Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Chicago Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule service around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, keeping downtime short, and staging temporary hot water where possible.

Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Chicago Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Chicago Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Chicago and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Chicago Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Call us to talk through a plan sized to your property and budget.
